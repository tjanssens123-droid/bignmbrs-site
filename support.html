BIGnmbrs support

Get started with your large numeric keyboard in a few steps.

Setup help

  1. Install and open BIGnmbrs on your iPhone.
  2. Go to Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards.
  3. Tap Add New Keyboard and choose BIGnmbrs.
  4. Open an ordinary text field, hold the globe key and select BIGnmbrs.

Frequently asked questions

Why can’t I use it in a payment or password field?

iOS uses the system keyboard in secure fields. Some apps also block third-party keyboards.

Do I need to enable Full Access?

No. BIGnmbrs is designed to work without Full Access.

How can I change key size?

Open BIGnmbrs settings and choose a larger keyboard layout.
