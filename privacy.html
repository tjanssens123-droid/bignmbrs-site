Privacy Policy

BIGnmbrs is a custom numeric keyboard for iPhone. This policy describes the intended app behavior and must be verified against the final release before publication.

Before publishing: verify this policy against the final app and add an approved support email address.

Keyboard input

While typing, the keyboard extension passes your input to the active text field on your iPhone. The app is designed not to record, analyze or transmit this input to an external server.

On-device preferences

Preferences such as keyboard size, appearance and decimal separator may be saved locally on your device to personalize the experience.

Accounts, advertising and analytics

The planned release requires no account and contains no advertising or tracking. Before publishing, confirm the final build contains no third-party analytics or tracking software.

Keyboard access and secure fields

BIGnmbrs is intended to function without the iOS Full Access permission. iOS may disable custom keyboards in secure text fields. Other apps may also restrict them.
